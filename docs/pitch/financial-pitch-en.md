## 1) Hiring — economic effect (1,000-employee company)

> **[Show charts/hiring-roi.html]**
>
> «Picture a company with **a thousand employees**. Every year it hires about **150 people**. For each
> opening, HR runs around **20 first-round interviews — and rejects 9 out of 10**.
>
> That's **3,000 interviews a year**, an hour each: roughly **one and a half full-time recruiters** doing
> almost nothing but saying no. In Colombia, about **COP 114 million a year** — most of it spent
> interviewing people who were going to be rejected anyway.
>
> The bottleneck isn't deciding who to hire. **It's filtering.**
>
> Apex Vision automates that first round: our AI agent **interviews every candidate in real time** — it
> listens, follows up, scores communication, posture and answers — and **only the top 25% ever reach a human**.
>
> Here's the unit that matters: a recruiter's first interview costs about **COP 38,000**. Our AI pre-screen
> costs about **COP 10,000** — nearly **four times cheaper**, instant, 24/7, and objective.
>
> For the company, human interviews drop from **3,000 to 750**, they free **2,250 recruiter-hours**, and the
> process falls from **COP 114M to COP 59M — about COP 55 million saved a year, roughly half**.
>
> And we **charge per use**: every minute of AI conversation is metered in Apex Tokens. The client pays only
> for what it consumes, on plans from **US$29/mo**. Our cost per token is a fraction of the price —
> **gross margin near 87%**. One large client like this is **~COP 30 million a year in recurring revenue**.»

**Hiring assumptions**

| Variable | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Hires / year | 150 | ~15% turnover + growth |
| First-round interviews / hire | 20 | typical funnel |
| Total human interviews / year | 3,000 | 150 × 20 |
| Loaded recruiter cost | COP 38,000/h | ~COP 6M/mo ÷ ~160 productive h |
| **Cost today** | **COP 114M** (~US$27k) | 3,000 × COP 38,000 |
| AI pre-screen | 8 min live = **24 AT** | live = 3 AT/min |
| Client AT price (Scale) | COP 420 (US$0.10) | financial plan §7 |
| **AI cost / candidate** | **~COP 10,000** | 24 × COP 420 |
| % advancing to human | 25% | top scorers only |
| **Cost with Apex** | **COP 59M** | AI COP 30M + 750 h × COP 38k |
| **Saving** | **~COP 55M (−48%)** | 114 − 59 |
| Revenue / client | **~COP 30M/yr** | 72,000 AT × COP 420 |

## 2) Sales — economic effect (100-rep sales team)

> **[Show charts/sales-roi.html]**
>
> «The other side: **training sellers**. Today a company trains with expensive courses and manager
> role-plays. Apex Vision lets **every rep practice unlimited pitches** against AI buyers — customer,
> director, tough negotiator — with an **objective score** and feedback. They ramp faster and close more.
>
> A team of **100 reps** brings in, say, **COP 60 billion a year**. This isn't cost savings — it's **new revenue**.
>
> With just **+1 point of win rate**, that team adds **+COP 600 million a year**. And the **25 new reps** who
> join each year ramp **a month sooner** → another **~COP 625 million** of accelerated revenue.
>
> The cost? Each AI practice runs about **COP 10,000** — versus an hour of a coach or manager — and it's
> unlimited, 24/7. The whole team practicing is **~COP 48 million a year**.
>
> So: **invest tens of millions, add thousands of millions**. For every peso in Apex Vision, the team
> generates **more than twelve** in revenue — and the meter never stops.»

**Sales assumptions**

| Variable | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Reps | 100 | |
| Revenue / rep | COP 600M/yr | typical B2B quota |
| Team revenue | **COP 60,000M/yr** (~US$14.3M) | 100 × 600M |
| New reps / year | 25 | ~25% turnover |
| Ramp acceleration | −1 month (5→4) | continuous AI practice |
| Ramp value | **~COP 625M/yr** | 25 × ~COP 25M |
| Win-rate uplift | +1 pt = **+COP 600M** | 1% of COP 60,000M (+2 pts ≈ +COP 1,200M) |

> Honesty note: in Colombia (lower wages, AI cost in USD) the **hiring** case saves **~48% / ~3×**; the
> **sales** case is revenue-side with far more upside (12×+). The most defensible hook is always the
> **per-unit** one: COP 10,000 with AI vs COP 38,000 human per candidate, and *a small team can
> filter/train 10× more without hiring anyone else*.
